An Asheville Day Trip from Waynesville: Three Ways to Plan It

By Mojo Manor · Updated September 7, 2026

All travel guidesLocal GuidePlan a stay at the Manor ↗

Asheville can be a rewarding day out from Waynesville without becoming a race between attractions. Choose one main reason to go, add a meal and leave time for the drive and parking. These three versions help you build a day with enough room to enjoy it.

Option one: make Biltmore the main event

Biltmore requires a reserved house-entry time. Choose your date and ticket on the estate’s current website, checking what is included. The entry time is for the house, so allow additional time for arrival, parking and reaching the entrance.

Leave the rest of the visit for whichever gardens, grounds or other included experiences interest you. A relaxed lunch and time to explore can fill the day. Do not book a tight sequence of downtown activities around a headline “afternoon at Biltmore” without checking the distances within the estate.

Ticket prices, included experiences and transport arrangements can change. Use the arrival and transport details attached to your selected ticket.

Option two: art studios and a downtown afternoon

Start by choosing artists or galleries from the River Arts District’s current studio information. Studios set individual opening days and hours. Confirm the places you most want to visit rather than assuming every former studio address is open.

Pair those stops with lunch and a manageable part of downtown. Allow travel and parking between areas. Choose a few blocks or one shop you want to spend time in; a smaller plan leaves room to browse instead of continually checking the clock.

For a meal, check the restaurant’s own current menu, location and reservation options. A reservation at the place you most want to try can be the anchor for the rest of the afternoon.

Option three: gardens and a slower afternoon

The North Carolina Arboretum is another main destination, with gardens and trails to choose according to your interests and the day’s conditions. Check current visitor information, entry charges, hours and access before deciding on a route.

Build the visit around one garden or walking plan that suits the group, then add a meal on the way back. Keep an indoor alternative in mind if the weather changes. There is no need to combine the Arboretum, Biltmore and the River Arts District to make the trip worthwhile.

Plan the drive and parking for the destination

Navigate to the actual first stop, not just “Asheville.” Route conditions, traffic and time spent parking affect the journey from Waynesville. Allow a buffer for a timed house entry, show or restaurant reservation; each destination has its own approach and parking arrangements.

Use the City of Asheville’s current garage information when parking downtown. Rates and rules differ between city, county and private facilities; do not assume every garage includes free hours. Check the signs at the garage you use.

Choose the evening before it becomes another long day

If your plans include drinks, arrange a sober driver or a confirmed ride for the return to Waynesville. Do not depend on an unverified late-evening rideshare being available. A daytime outing and dinner back in Waynesville can be just as satisfying as staying in Asheville late.

Mojo Manor brings you back to a residential town setting, a private backyard and a game room. Explore the house photos and compare the local brewery options if you would rather keep the next day close to home.
