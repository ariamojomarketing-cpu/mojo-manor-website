Group Vacation Rentals in Waynesville, NC: What to Check Before Booking

By Mojo Manor · Updated September 7, 2026

All travel guidesLocal GuidePlan a stay at the Manor ↗

The right group rental is the one that fits how your group will sleep, spend time together and get around. Start with the floor plan and the full booking total, then look at the experiences you want nearby. A clear plan before booking makes the stay easier for everyone.

Assign sleeping spaces before you reserve

Mojo Manor accommodates up to ten registered guests with four upstairs bedrooms and an additional futon in the downstairs game room. The futon is in a shared entertainment space, not a fifth bedroom. Agree who will sleep where and how evening games will work if someone is using that space.

There are 2.5 bathrooms: both full bathrooms are upstairs and a half bath is downstairs. A downstairs futon does not create a step-free bedroom-and-shower arrangement. Review the bedroom and bathroom photographs and ask about any specific mobility needs before committing.

Look for places to gather and places to separate

Two living areas, a stocked kitchen and a game room give the group more than one place to be. Some people can cook while others play table tennis or settle in for a film. There is room for a shared dinner and for a quieter corner afterward.

The 45-second house film gives a quick introduction; use the gallery for the practical details. Plan groceries, shared meals and any equipment you depend on before arrival. Guests use their own streaming accounts.

Choose a home that fits different kinds of days

Mojo Manor is in a residential Waynesville neighborhood, one mile from downtown. It suits a trip that mixes town meals and shopping with separate mountain outings. Town restaurants and mountain outings can each have their own place in the weekend.

Use the Local Guide to compare routes. A group can split between a town morning and a hike, then meet back at the house. Check actual travel conditions; Asheville, the Smokies and the ski area are different journeys, not interchangeable quick stops.

A group stay still follows the house rules

The maximum is ten registered guests. Parties and events are not permitted. Quiet hours are 10 PM–8 AM, and outdoor music and conversation should be considerate of neighbors. Plan reunions and friends’ weekends around the registered group.

The private driveway fits up to six cars. Agree your transport plans before arrival, particularly if people will be returning from different outings. Arrange a sober driver or confirmed ride before any brewery visit.

Compare the complete stay total

Enter your dates, guest count and dogs to see the itemized quote. Rates and minimum stays vary. Review cleaning, other fees, taxes, payment dates and cancellation terms together; a nightly headline price alone does not tell you the cost of the trip. The Rates & Policies page explains the house’s current arrangements.

Up to two dogs are welcome, with a $100 fee per dog per stay. Include them in the booking details. Check-in is after 4 PM and checkout by 11 AM. Early arrival or late departure must be arranged and is not a guaranteed direct-booking perk.

A simple way to make the decision together

Share the gallery and sleeping plan with everyone. Confirm the full price split, cancellation terms, transport, dog arrangements and who needs a quiet sleeping space. Then choose one or two shared activities and leave the rest of the itinerary flexible.

The appeal is time together with room for different paces: coffee on the porch, a game after dinner, a day beyond town and a comfortable home to return to. Make sure that version of the trip matches the group before you reserve.
