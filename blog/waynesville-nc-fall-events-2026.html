Fall 2026 Events in Waynesville & Maggie Valley

Apple Harvest Festival — October 17

The Haywood Chamber lists the 2026 Apple Harvest Festival for Saturday, October 17, in downtown Waynesville. The free street festival brings apple-themed food, arts and crafts to Main Street.

Mojo Manor is one mile from downtown. Plan parking and transportation around the event's street arrangements rather than assuming the drive or walk will take a fixed number of minutes. A downtown day pairs well with an unhurried evening back at the house.

Maggie Valley festival dates

The Town of Maggie Valley's official schedule lists these fall events:

  • September 11–13: Thunder in the Smokies Fall Rally.
  • September 18–19: VWs in the Valley.
  • September 24–26: Maggie Valley Swap Meet & C10 Nationals.
  • October 2–3: Vairs in the Valley.
  • October 9–11: Southeast All Truck Nationals.
  • October 17–18: Maggie Valley Arts & Crafts Show.

Check the individual organizer's information before booking tickets or deciding which day to attend. Some events are designed around particular vehicles or activities; choose the one that interests your group. For the motorcycle rally, see the Thunder in the Smokies event page.

Make a weekend of it

Friday: arrive after 4 PM, get settled and pick one easy dinner plan. The house has a stocked kitchen and two living areas if your group prefers to stay in.

Saturday: make the festival your main outing. Bring what you need for the weather and leave time for traffic, parking and a meal. Back at the Manor, the game room and hot tub let the evening continue without another reservation.

Sunday: choose a short town stop around your 11 AM checkout, or book a longer stay if you want a separate day for the mountains. Fitting a major scenic drive into a departure morning can turn a relaxed weekend into a rush.

Pairing festivals with fall color

Festival dates are scheduled; leaf color is not. Read our fall foliage planning guide and check the Parkway's road status before choosing a route. Keep an indoor or downtown option for unsettled weather.

Check the house for your dates
