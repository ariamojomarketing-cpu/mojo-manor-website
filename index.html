BEFORE YOU PACK

Good to know.

Rates & policies ↗
Is the house in the mountains or in town?+

Mojo Manor is in a residential neighborhood in Waynesville, about one mile from downtown. It is a convenient home base for mountain adventures, with restaurants, shops and everyday essentials nearby. It is not a secluded mountaintop cabin.

How many people can stay?+

The home welcomes up to 10 guests, with four upstairs bedrooms and two separate living rooms downstairs. There are two full bathrooms upstairs and a half bath downstairs. All bedrooms require stairs.

Can we bring our dog?+

Dogs are welcome, subject to the pet policy and fees shown when you book. The house has a fenced backyard, plus bowls and a dog bed. Please include your dogs in your booking details.

What is provided?+

Bed linens, towels, a stocked kitchen, a coffee station, Wi-Fi, Smart TVs, a washer and dryer, and access to the house’s game room and outdoor amenities. Ask us about children’s equipment before arrival.

Where do we see the total and cancellation terms?+

Choose your dates in the booking window to see current availability and the stay total. Review the fee breakdown, pet charges and cancellation terms in the booking engine before confirming. Our Rates & Policies page has additional information.

Are parties or events allowed?+

Mojo Manor is a home in a residential neighborhood. Parties and events are not permitted. Please follow the house rules and quiet hours provided with your reservation.
