A feel for the whole stay.

This 45-second film brings together photographs of Mojo Manor: the living spaces, kitchen, bedrooms, games and backyard. The house is in a residential Waynesville neighborhood, about one mile from downtown, with mountain adventures within reach.

  • Four bedrooms upstairs; up to ten guests across the home.
  • Two full bathrooms upstairs and a half bath downstairs.
  • Two living rooms, an electric fireplace, karaoke and a stocked kitchen.
  • A private arcade, hot tub, fenced backyard and outdoor games.

Use the full photo gallery for individual rooms and practical details, and Rates & Policies to plan your stay. English automatic captions are available through the YouTube player's CC control.
